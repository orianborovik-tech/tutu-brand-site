## 01 · The Nested Crater

**Description:**
An aerial view of a massive impact crater with concentric geological rings descending toward a glowing golden core. The surrounding landscape is tinted alien green with yellow mineral veins tracing the crater walls. A second smaller crater sits at the center, lit from within.

**Seedance 2.0 Prompt:**
```
Slow drone descends toward a concentric impact crater, each ring of eroded rock glowing gold and amber. Alien green light washes the surrounding plains. Camera spirals inward toward the luminous core. Cinematic, wide lens, god rays cutting through haze, otherworldly.
```
- `--aspect_ratio 16:9` · `--duration 10`
