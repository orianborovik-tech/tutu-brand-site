# Dot — Environment Reference (15 Scenes)

A description of each of the 15 environment plates. All scenes share a consistent
visual world: an alien / extraterrestrial wasteland under a glowing **green nebula
sky**, with terrain pushed toward **toxic yellow, ochre and sulphur tones**.
Cinematic, wide-format, atmospheric. Useful as a backdrop / mood library for the
brand world.

**Shared palette & mood**
- Sky: vivid emerald-green nebula, star fields, cyan/teal accents, occasional
  orange-pink wisps.
- Ground: yellow-ochre, sulphur, sand and bare rock, often hazy or steaming.
- Lighting: glowing, otherworldly, high contrast between green sky and warm ground.
- Format: cinematic widescreen (16:9), scene/landscape compositions, no people.

---

## 1. Green-Lit Rocky Plain
A low, sweeping desert plain of yellow sand scattered with dark boulders in the
foreground. A soft dune ridge curves across the midground. Above it, a luminous
green nebula glows from horizon to top, with a brighter cyan-blue swirl in the
upper-right corner. Calm, vast, alien-dawn feeling.

## 2. Lava Pit on the Yellow Plain
The same yellow-sulphur terrain, but a glowing **pool of molten lava** sits in a
shallow crater at center, with orange splashes and rising heat. The green nebula
sky behind is softer and dustier, dotted with distant stars and a blue accent at
right. Heat-and-cold contrast: fiery foreground against the cool green void.

## 3. Cracked Flats with Steam Vents
A flat, pale cracked salt-pan / clay floor stretching to the horizon. Thin
**white steam columns** rise vertically across the midground. The upper half is
dominated by a dense, swirling green nebula. Eerie, geothermal, desolate.

## 4. Forest of Stone Spires
A dramatic field of tall, pointed **rock pinnacles / spires** rising from a
cracked plateau, with a deep chasm cutting through the middle. A brilliant
**green star-flare** bursts on the horizon, casting a cross-shaped lens flare.
Epic, cathedral-like, the most heroic of the set.

## 5. Crater Mouth over a Nebula Abyss
A close view down into a large **crater / pit**, its near rim in shadow. Inside
and beyond, a glowing green nebula seems to fill the void like a window into deep
space. Bright green-yellow light spills over the far rim. Mysterious, portal-like.

## 6. The "GENPALIOINSS" Bunker
A massive **circular industrial structure / bunker** of riveted metal panels
half-buried in yellow earth. A large round emblem — split green/orange with a
stylized planet-and-ring logo and the lettering "GENPALIOINSS" — sits at center.
Green nebula and stars fill the sky. The one man-made / branded set in the world.

## 7. Honeycomb Salt Field (Aerial)
A low-angle look across a vast field of **hexagonal yellow-orange plates** (like
a dried honeycomb salt flat) receding to a hazy horizon. Faint steam drifts up.
Moody blue-grey sky above instead of vivid green. Geometric, hypnotic, warm.

## 8. Green Grand Canyon
A monumental layered **canyon / gorge** of stepped cliffs and ridges, lit in
gold and olive-green raking light, deep shadow in the foreground ravine. A
turbulent green sky sits low on the horizon. Grand, geological, painterly.

## 9. Cracked Hex Desert under Storm Nebula
A foreground of large **cracked hexagonal plates** of dry earth, glowing faintly
warm along the seams. Behind, a powerful **green-and-cyan nebula storm** churns
across the upper sky with a darker cloud core. Dramatic, ominous, weather-like.

## 10. Lava-Seamed Hex Plateau
A wide plateau of **hexagonal slabs separated by glowing molten-lava seams**,
receding toward a distant mountain. The sky is a heavy green nebula with a bright
blue crystalline flare in the upper-right. Volcanic, cracked-crust, energetic.

## 11. Twin Mushroom Pillars
Two huge eroded **mushroom/goblet-shaped rock pillars** stand on a stony plain,
each venting a plume of **blue-teal smoke** into a saturated green sky with
orange-pink wisps. Surreal, totem-like, almost a vintage color-film look.

## 12. Concentric Terraced Crater
An aerial view of a deep **stepped crater of concentric rings**, golden-yellow
terraces spiraling down to a bright pit at center. The surrounding land is washed
in green. Resembles a mine or impact basin — engineered yet natural, mesmerizing.

## 13. Terraced Yellow Pools
Inside a green-misted gorge, a series of **stepped pools of glowing yellow-gold
liquid** cascade across the floor, framed by wet ochre rock ledges and faint
pillars. Mineral hot-spring / alien spa mood. Wet, reflective, luminous.

## 14. Green-Sky Dunes
Rolling **sand dunes** in warm yellow-gold, ridgelines catching light, rippling
to a low horizon. Above, a brooding **green storm sky** with hazy clouds.
Quieter and more naturalistic — a classic desert under an alien atmosphere.

## 15. Reflective Shore under the Nebula
A near-symmetrical **water's edge** where a thin line of golden rocks meets a
mirror-still pool that reflects the sky. Above, a rich green nebula with a
brown-and-cyan dust column rising at center. Tranquil, dreamlike, the most
reflective/serene composition of the set.
